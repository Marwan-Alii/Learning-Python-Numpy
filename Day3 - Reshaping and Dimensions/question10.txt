# QUESTION 10 - EXPLAIN IN YOUR OWN WORDS

1. What reshape does?
    Reshape takes the original array, and changes its structures.
    Note: The new row and col should be such that newrow * newcol = size

2. Difference between Reshape and Transpose
    Reshape changes the dimensions of the Array into desired ones
    Transpose just switches the Rows with Columns

3. What Axis means in NumPy
    Axis means a single dimension of Multi-Dimensional Array.
    axis=0 mean vertical
    axis=1 mean horizontal
    higher axis is for (n>=3)d arrays
